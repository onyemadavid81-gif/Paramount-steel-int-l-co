Orile Iganmu Logistics Hub

High-Capacity Stockyard & Material Distribution

Situated at the heart of Lagos' industrial corridor in Orile Iganmu, Paramount Steel operates a heavy-duty stockyard and warehousing facility equipped for rapid offloading, inventory indexing, and heavy transport dispatch.

Our depot maintains an extensive buffer stock of heavy universal beams, mild steel plates, seamless pipes, and commercial profiles. Positioned adjacent to key transport corridors and Lagos seaport access roads, our facilities ensure frictionless movement from mill delivery to project sites across West Africa.

Depot Operations & Equipment

Stock Staging & Storage

Dedicated indoor and open-air bays systematically organized by gauge, material grade, and heat numbers for effortless traceability and quick loading.

Custom Material Prep

Onsite gas cutting, profile sawing, and slitting equipment available to prep stock to client specification prior to dispatch, eliminating extra site overhead.

Heavy Crane & Rigging

Overhead traveling cranes and heavy forklifts capable of handling bundled seamless pipes, multi-ton plate stacks, and 12m+ beam lengths safely.
