Who We Are

Nigeria's Direct Importer & Heavy Steel Stockist

Paramount Steel International Co. is a market-leading importer, stockholder, and major supplier of high-grade structural steel products in Nigeria. Headquartered strategically in Lagos, we provide foundational steel resources for civil engineering, marine construction, oilfield fabrications, and commercial development projects.

Established with an unyielding dedication to structural integrity, our stockyard in Orile Iganmu houses thousands of metric tons of certified steel plates, universal beams, seamless piping systems, and specialized industrial alloys sourced directly from world-class ISO-certified steel mills.

"Our mission is simple: to empower engineering and construction across West Africa by guaranteeing direct stock availability, exact dimensional compliance, and uncompromised structural reliability."
