Metallurgical Integrity & Structural Safeguards

At Paramount Steel International Co., quality compliance is treated as a critical safety mandate rather than a procedural formality. For high-load civil superstructures, offshore energy rigs, and marine assets, structural failure is unacceptable.

Every tonnage unit entering our Orile Iganmu logistics hub undergoes rigorous mechanical stress verification, chemical analysis, and dimensional calibration. By maintaining direct procurement links with tier-1 ISO/EN certified global steel mills, we guarantee complete supply chain traceability from blast furnace chemistry to project site erection.

Mill Certification & Heat Traceability

Every profile, plate, and seamless pipe is delivered with authentic EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificates (MTC), verifying exact chemical compositions, yield points, tensile strength, and elongation performance.

Micrometer & Gauge Calibration

Our QA team conducts strict dimensional audits—measuring web/flange tolerances, wall uniformity on seamless pipes, and exact plate gauges—to ensure seamless alignment with engineering drawings.

Non-Destructive Surface Inspection

Stock undergoes thorough visual and ultrasonic non-destructive testing (NDT) to screen for internal lamination, micro-fissures, surface pitting, or oxidation prior to stock placement or dispatch.
