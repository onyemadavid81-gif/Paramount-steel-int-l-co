About Paramount Steel

Engineering Strength.
Delivering Trust.

Paramount Steel International Co. is a premier importer and stockist of mild steel plates, universal beams, channels, seamless pipes, and structural profiles engineered for infrastructure, construction, oilfields, and heavy fabrications.

With our primary depots situated at Orile Iganmu, Lagos, we deliver precision-built steel solutions that adhere strictly to international mill specifications and ISO compliance standards.
